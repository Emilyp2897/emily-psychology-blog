## Instagram / Facebook (main post)

**Caption:**

Mind the Gael is live.

It is a website about the mental side of sport for female athletes. Sports psychology, practical tools, mental health, and short mindfulness practices. All of it free.

I am not a psychologist. I am a 32 year old playing Ladies Gaelic Football for Holloway Gaels, and I spent about fifteen years not understanding what was going on in my own head. Anxiety, depression, panic attacks on the pitch, and a long stretch of drinking to avoid all of it. I walked away from sport at 17 because of pressure and politics and it took me seven years to come back.

Nobody handed me the language for any of that. That is the gap this is trying to close.

What is on it:

Training the Mind, twelve topics in sports psychology
The Gael Performance Toolkit, one set of practical tools for each topic
Stronger Minds, Stronger Players, the mental health side
Mindfulness and Affirmations, five minutes or less

Everything is free to read. There is no sign up and no subscription.

mindthegael.co.uk

If any of it is useful to one person who is where I was, that will do.
